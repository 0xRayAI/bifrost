# Suit to Bifrost and back

The posted diagram is [Suit to Bifrost and back](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HW7_a0EXgAAGV7_?format=png&name=large). Cyan is the forward path. Gold is the return.

The suit carries Constitution, OP-PROC, Activity, Station, Repertoire, Goggles, and Codex.

One turn:

1. Next line
2. Start
3. Do the work
4. Look again
5. End the turn
6. Hand it on

Git:

1. Issue or pull
2. Open the pull
3. Push
4. Review
5. Checks pass
6. Merge

Bifrost:

1. Collector
2. Card
3. Post
4. Queue
5. Offer
6. Wake

Under the board: Watchdog, Health, Governor, Station nudge, Nudge, Follow-up.

Gold returns: hand it on into Git, back to the queue into Next line, and one more ring from Follow-up.

A packet on the card has goal, constraints, path, acceptance, gates, evidence, next owner, escalate, and raci. R is who does the work. A is who answers. A live card missing R or A is amber. Scope is another name for goal.
